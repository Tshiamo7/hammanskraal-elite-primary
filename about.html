About Our School

Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence in Education

Hammanskraal Elite School was established in January 2000 by Mrs Esme Penaar with just 30 learners from Grade 1 to 7. In September 2010, Mr Eric Semenya acquired the school and by January 2014, it had grown into a well-established institution.

Due to growing numbers, a new campus was leased to accommodate the Intermediate and Senior Phase (Grade 4–7), while the original campus remained dedicated to the Foundation Phase (Grade RR–3) with 8 classrooms. Both campuses are uniquely situated within the Dinokeng Game Reserve, where learners experience the tranquility of nature and can spot the Big 5 on their way to school.

Today, with 274 learners and 14 dedicated staff members across two campuses, we continue our mission of providing quality education in one of the most unique school settings in Gauteng. Our commitment to academic excellence, character development, and celebrating our rich South African heritage remains at the heart of everything we do.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To provide a safe, nurturing, and supportive learning environment where every child can discover their unique potential, develop strong moral values, and acquire the knowledge and skills needed to succeed in life. We are committed to excellence in teaching, underpinned by transparent communication, while celebrating our South African heritage and preparing learners to become responsible citizens and future leaders.

🌟 Our Vision

To be the leading primary school in our community, recognized for academic excellence, character development, and cultural pride. We envision a school where every child feels valued, inspired, and empowered to reach their full potential. Our goal is to produce well-rounded individuals who will make positive contributions to society.
