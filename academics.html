Academic Programme

Our Approach

A Curriculum Built for Excellence

At Hammanskraal Elite Primary School, we follow the CAPS (Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement) curriculum as prescribed by the Department of Basic Education. Our primary medium of instruction is English (Home Language), with Afrikaans integrated as the mandatory First Additional Language. All academic instruction, assessments, and official correspondence are conducted in English.

We believe in developing the whole child – academically, socially, emotionally, and physically. Our balanced approach ensures that learners not only excel in their studies but also develop critical thinking skills, creativity, and a love for learning. The school utilises a system of continuous assessment to monitor and report on learner progress.

Small class sizes allow our teachers to provide individualized attention, ensuring that every learner receives the support they need to reach their full potential. Learners are responsible for the completion of all classwork, homework, and formal assessments.

Grade Levels

Our Academic Phases

Pre-Primary Phase

Grade RR - R

In the Pre-Primary phase, children learn foundational skills through play-based, interactive activities, focusing on holistic development. The goal is to build a strong base for formal schooling by fostering curiosity, social awareness, and key cognitive abilities.

  • Literacy (letters, sounds, early reading)
  • Numeracy (counting, shapes)
  • Life Skills (social skills, emotional regulation, arts, music, movement)
  • Physical Development (gross/fine motor skills)
Foundation Phase

Grade 1 - 3

The Foundation Phase teaches core subjects delivered through an integrated, practical approach to build foundational literacy, numeracy, and 21st-century skills.

  • English Home Language
  • Afrikaans First Additional Language
  • Mathematics
  • Life Skills (PE, art, music, social development)
Intermediate Phase

Grade 4 - 6

In the Intermediate Phase, students learn a broader range of core subjects with a greater focus on developing technical and academic skills, independence, and self-discovery.

  • English Home Language
  • Afrikaans First Additional Language
  • Mathematics
  • Natural Sciences
  • Social Sciences
  • Life Skills (PE, Art, Music, Bible)
Senior Phase

Grade 7

In the Senior Phase, students continue with core subjects. Economic Management Sciences is added in Grade 7. This phase is crucial in preparing learners for high school.

  • English
  • Afrikaans
  • Mathematics
  • Natural Sciences
  • Social Sciences (History & Geography)
  • Technology
  • Life Orientation
  • Arts & Culture
  • Economic Management Sciences
