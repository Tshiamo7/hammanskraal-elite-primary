Admission Requirements

Required Documents

  • Certified copy of learner's birth certificate
  • Certified copy of parent/guardian ID
  • Proof of residence (utility bill or lease agreement)
  • Previous school reports (if applicable)
  • Transfer card from previous school (if applicable)
  • Immunization records
  • Two recent passport-size photos

Age Requirements

  • Grade R: Turning 5 by 30 June of the year of admission
  • Grade 1: Turning 6 by 30 June of the year of admission
  • Grade 2-7: Age-appropriate placement based on previous schooling
  • Assessment may be required for placement
  • Late applications considered based on availability

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the school year start?

Our school year follows the national school calendar, typically starting in mid-January and ending in early December. We have four terms with school holidays in between.

What are the school hours and attendance policy?

School hours are from 7:30 AM to 2:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Learners are required to attend school daily and arrive before the commencement of the first period. Any absence must be substantiated by a written explanation or medical certificate from a parent/guardian. Persistent late arrivals or unexcused absenteeism will result in a formal report to the parents and potential disciplinary review. The school office is open from 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM for enquiries and administrative matters.

Do you offer after-care services?

Yes, we offer after-care services until 5:00 PM for an additional fee. This includes supervised homework time, snacks, and recreational activities.

What is your learner-to-teacher ratio?

We maintain small class sizes with a maximum of 35 learners per class, ensuring each child receives adequate attention and support from our teachers. Learners must maintain a high level of respect for educators, support staff, and peers. Bullying, physical altercations, vandalism, and insubordination are strictly prohibited.

Is there a school uniform?

Yes, the prescribed school uniform must be worn in its entirety every school day. Uniforms must be clean, pressed, and in good repair. Any temporary deviation from the uniform code requires a written explanation from a parent or guardian for administrative approval. Details about the uniform and where to purchase it will be provided upon admission.
