Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How do I schedule a school tour?

You can schedule a school tour by calling our office, sending an email, or filling out the contact form above. We typically respond within 24 hours to arrange a convenient time.

What documents do I need for admission?

You'll need the learner's birth certificate, parent/guardian ID, proof of residence, previous school reports (if applicable), and immunization records.

Do you offer transport services?

Currently, we do not provide transport services. Our school is located within the Dinokeng Game Reserve. Many parents organize private transport groups. Contact us for more information.

Can I visit during school hours?

Yes, visitors are welcome during school hours. All visitors must report to the administrative office and sign the register before entering school grounds. For tours, we recommend scheduling in advance. Please note that dangerous objects, weapons, or illegal substances are strictly forbidden on the premises.
