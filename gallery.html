Testimonials

Her academic growth and personal development over the past few years wouldn't have been possible without Elite Primary School. Teachers are amazing and considerate when addressing any concerns parents have. Their approach is very positive. All in all, I am pleased that our children are in the hands of good teachers and other members of Elite Primary School. Positive connections between parents and teachers have been shown to improve learners' academic achievement through meetings, consultation days where parents engage with different teachers of different subjects to ensure the progress of the child. I asked my child what her thought is about her school, and she said: "We get more freedom, and during recess, instead of avoiding teachers we approach them to discuss matters that we don't understand." Their relationship with teachers is incredible. Our kids are taking on more responsibilities willingly and show leadership qualities. Thanks to our teachers who stepped up to be our children's guardians and best friends.
